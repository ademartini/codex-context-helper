# Community expectations

Be respectful, assume good intent, and keep feedback specific to the work. People of all backgrounds and experience levels are welcome.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, spam, and publishing another person's private information are not acceptable. Respect requests to stop unwanted contact.

Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, or restrict participation to protect the community. Use GitHub's reporting tools for abuse. Never publish private information as evidence in an issue.
